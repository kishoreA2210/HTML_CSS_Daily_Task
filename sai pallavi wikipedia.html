Sai Pallavi

Natural actor in Tamil Cinema

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia In this Indian name, the name Kannan is a patronymic, and the person should be referred to by the given name, Sai Pallavi Senthamarai.

Sai Pallavi Senthamarai Kannan (pronounced [saɪ paˈləvi]; born 9 May 1992) is an Indian actress who primarily works in Tamil and Telugu cinema. Pallavi has received several accolades, including a Tamil Nadu State Film Award, seven Filmfare Awards South and three SIIMA Awards. She was honoured with Kalaimamani in the year 2021 by the Government of Tamil Nadu.[3]
Pallavi's acting journey began with uncredited roles and dance show appearances, but her breakthrough came as Malar in the 2015 Malayalam film Premam. While a medical student, her acclaimed performance earned her a Filmfare Award for Best Female Debut – South, launching her into the South Indian film industry. In 2017, she ventured into Telugu cinema with Fidaa, winning the Filmfare Award for Best Actress – Telugu.[4]
Pallavi has starred in the films Kali (2016), Middle Class Abbayi (2017), Maari 2 (2018), Paava Kadhaigal (2020), Love Story (2021), Shyam Singha Roy (2021), Gargi (2022), Amaran (2024) and Thandel (2025). Her song "Rowdy Baby" is the first South Indian video song to reach one billion views on YouTube.[5]
Her performances in Gargi, Amaran and Love Story earned the Filmfare Award for Best Actress in Tamil and Telugu, respectively, while her work in Shyam Singha Roy and Virata Parvam (2022) gained her the Filmfare Critics Award for Best Actress – Telugu.[6] She was featured by Forbes magazine as one of India's 30 Under 30 in 2020.[7]

Early life and work

Sai Pallavi was born on 9 May 1992[8] in a Badaga family[9] in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Her hometown is Kotagiri in the Nilgiris district, Tamil Nadu. She was born to Senthamarai Kannan, a central excise officer (often associated with police and customs work) and a football player, and Radha Kannan, a dancer. Pallavi herself is an avid badminton player. She has a younger sister, Pooja Kannan, who has also followed in her footsteps, working as an actress in small roles and making her debut in the Tamil film Chithirai Sevvaanam which was released in the year 2023. Pallavi was raised and educated in Coimbatore, doing her schooling at Avila Convent School.[10][11][12] She pursued a career in medicine, graduating in 2016 from the Tbilisi State Medical University in Georgia, which is recognised by the Medical Council of India.[13][14] She successfully took her Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE) on 31 August 2020 in Trichy.[15] [16] However, despite officially being a doctor, she has not yet registered as a medical practitioner in India , choosing instead to focus entirely on her flourishing career as an actress.[17] Besides her native Badaga, Pallavi is multilingual and speaks Tamil, English, Telugu, Hindi and Georgian.[18]
